Output 1

The one-page offer summary

Everything that matters about your offer on a single page. Send it to a prospect before a call, use it as the outline of your sales page, or pin it above your desk.

  • Program name, tagline and your one-sentence promise
  • Who it's for and the problem it solves
  • Your method in 3 phases
  • What's included and what they'll walk away with
  • Pricing, who it's for and who it's not for
  • Your bio and a clear call to action

Edit any line, then save it as a PDF in one click.

Output 2

The full offer doc

A complete, beautifully designed offer document that maps out everything a buyer needs to know before they say yes. Usually 5 pages.

  • Cover with your program name and promise
  • The opportunity: where they are now, what staying stuck costs, where they could be
  • Your method: 3 phases, 9 steps and a week-by-week path
  • What they get: every deliverable explained, plus outcomes
  • Investment and FAQ: pricing options and answers to their real objections
Output 3

Your AI context file: CLAUDE.md or ChatGPT

Every time you open a new chat, your AI starts from zero. It doesn't know who you serve, what you sell or how you talk, so you get generic answers that sound like everyone else.

Your AI context file fixes that. It packs your Purpose, Prospect, Problem, Promise, Process, Profit and Program into one streamlined file, along with your offer and your own words. Add it once and every conversation starts already knowing your business.
